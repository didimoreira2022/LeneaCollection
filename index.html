Cushions

Designed as a modular system, the cushions adapt to different moments and postures. Each form brings a distinct function while maintaining a cohesive and balanced aesthetic.

image 5
image 6

Dome, Wave, Zafu.

  • The Dome cushion features a semi-spherical shape, offering enveloping support and a sculptural presence.
  • The Wave cushion is designed to provide ergonomic back support, enhancing comfort in a natural, relaxed posture.
  • The Zafu is a flatter, more balanced cushion. Its low profile offers a sense of stability and simplicity.

Details

Guided Form

Subtle Signature

image 9

Diana Moreira

Diana Moreira is a furniture designer whose work follows a minimal and elegant approach, seeking a balance between comfort, adaptability, and formal clarity. LENEA Collection reflects this approach through a calm and intentional design, informed by a study of shape coding that guides the form, its perception, and the way it is experienced.

“LENEA Collection was born from the intention to create a quiet object — with a sculptural presence that supports the body and responds to the rhythms of everyday life.”

FAQ

How long will it take for my furniture order to be delivered?

Production takes up to 5 weeks before your piece is ready for shipment. Europe: please allow 4 to 7 business days for shipping. International: please allow 7 to 16 business days for shipping.
